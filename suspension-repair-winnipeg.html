Find out what’s causing the rough ride

Steering and suspension concerns can show up as noise, vibration, uneven tire wear or a change in handling. These symptoms can have more than one cause, so we inspect the vehicle before recommending a repair.

Shocks, struts and suspension repairs

We check the relevant steering and suspension components, including shocks and struts, based on the issue you describe. We explain what we find, the recommended work and the price before repairs begin.

Help us understand the symptom

Tell us whether the noise happens over bumps, when turning or at a certain speed. Include your vehicle’s year, make and model, plus any recent changes in ride quality or tire wear.
