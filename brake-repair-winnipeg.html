Get the cause checked before replacing parts

A new noise or a change in braking deserves attention. Tell us when the problem happens—during light braking, at higher speeds or every time you stop—so we can focus the inspection on your concern.

Brake inspections, pads and rotors

We inspect the brake system and discuss the condition of the components before recommending work. Depending on what we find, the repair may involve brake pads, rotors or another part of the system. We explain the recommended repair and price before work begins.

What to tell us when you call

Have your vehicle’s year, make and model ready. Mention any brake warning light, noise, vibration or change in pedal feel, along with when you first noticed it.
